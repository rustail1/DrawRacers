# 39 — PLAYER SAFETY & DRAWING MODERATION
Статус: **SAFETY CONTRACT v1.3.4**.

## Risk
Freehand geometry can be misused to create offensive/sexual/hateful symbols visible to others. Core benefit requires creative shapes, so safety is designed into reach and lifetime.

## Project decisions
- drawings are ephemeral gameplay input, not saved/shared art;
- only final rotating leg geometry is visible to rivals, not the 2D drawing canvas;
- shape lifetime is limited to the heat/redraw;
- no gallery, naming, comments, likes or persistent public publication;
- setting allows reducing/hiding detailed rival custom shapes and using simplified leg presentation;
- standard Roblox player reporting remains available; safety metrics reviewed after launch.

## Escalation
If abuse reports show drawing channel risk, priority options are: reduce rival detail/reach, restrict topology/shape vocabulary, trust-based visibility, or pre-set safe shapes for affected cohorts. Product safety beats preserving unrestricted drawing.

## Compliance
Before public launch complete current Maturity & Compliance questionnaire and re-check Roblox safety guidance. Any future user-generated text must use platform-safe filtered paths; do not invent custom unfiltered chat.
