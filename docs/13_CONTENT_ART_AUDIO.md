## Clean-room rule
Не копировать Draw Climber branding, exact models, textures, colors, UI layout/art, sounds, music, exact level layouts or extracted assets. Берём functional cause, создаём собственное expression.

## 1. Visual direction
**Exact launch palette, typography, two themes, public title, cosmetic catalog, discovery compositions and asset checklist are owned by `62_LAUNCH_CONTENT_ART_DIRECTION_MANIFEST.md`. This file is the broad brief; artists do not invent a competing launch art direction.**

Goal: мгновенно читаемые silhouettes, яркая casual Roblox эстетика, low visual noise around collision surfaces.

### Racer
- canonical collision cube;
- cosmetic body skins must preserve collision envelope;
- racer face language is the fixed simple eyes/mouth system from `62`; sticker collage is used only by the named cosmetic in `62`;
- line/leg visual can be stylized independently from physical collider.

### Tracks
- gameplay collision geometry simple/readable;
- thematic decoration outside collision silhouette;
- color contrast separates track vs racer legs vs background.
