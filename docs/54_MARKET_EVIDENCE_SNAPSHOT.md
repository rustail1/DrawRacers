## Product conclusion
The evidence is sufficient to treat **functional drawing → physical/obstacle consequence** as a market-precedented pattern rather than a speculative mechanic. It is also sufficient for the Product Owner to lock this direction and proceed to execution.

The same evidence explicitly **does not prove** Draw Racers' 8-player social multiplier, rematch behavior, D1/D7, cosmetic desire, PTR, payer conversion, repeat spend or revenue. Those are our empirical product outcomes and are routed to `55`, `46`, soft launch and LiveOps.
